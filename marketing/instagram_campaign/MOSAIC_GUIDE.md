## Posting order — **reverse**

Instagram inserts new posts at the top-left of your profile grid and pushes
the existing posts right + down. To assemble the 3×3 mosaic you must post
**tile_09 first, then 08, 07, 06, 05, 04, 03, 02, and finally tile_01**.

After all 9 are up your profile reads exactly like the master canvas.

| Posting order | File | Lands in profile slot |
|---:|---|---|
| 1st post | `tile_09.png` | bottom-right |
| 2nd      | `tile_08.png` | bottom-centre |
| 3rd      | `tile_07.png` | bottom-left |
| 4th      | `tile_06.png` | middle-right |
| 5th      | `tile_05.png` | dead centre |
| 6th      | `tile_04.png` | middle-left |
| 7th      | `tile_03.png` | top-right |
| 8th      | `tile_02.png` | top-centre |
| 9th      | `tile_01.png` | top-left |

Pace them 1 per day for 9 consecutive days, OR drop 3 per day across 3 days
(post the bottom row in one session, then middle, then top — each session
unlocks a fully-finished horizontal stripe on the profile).

## Pro tips

- **Pin the bottom row** (`tile_09`, `tile_08`, `tile_07`) once they're up so they never get pushed off the visible profile grid by future posts.
- **Carousel companion:** drop the `mosaic_master.png` as a single carousel post on day 10 with the caption *"Here's the whole grid in one shot — save it"*. Drives massive saves & shares.
- **LinkedIn cross-post:** the master canvas works exceptionally well as a LinkedIn document post (PDF) — viewers swipe through all 9 panels in one feed unit.
- **Story re-shares:** every time you post a tile, immediately re-share to Stories with a "Tap to see the grid build →" sticker pointing at your profile link.
