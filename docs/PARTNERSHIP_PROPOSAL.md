## 1. Executive Summary

GymAxis AI has developed a white-label **Self-Service Maintenance Portal** purpose-built for gym equipment management. This proposal outlines a commercial partnership with Pulse Fitness to deploy the portal across Pulse Fitness's customer base in two tiers:

- **Pulse Self-Service (Lite)** — Bundled free with Pulse Fitness equipment
- **Pulse Self-Service Pro** — Premium subscription sold jointly

GymAxis AI retains full ownership of the GymAxis AI platform and all underlying intellectual property. Pulse Fitness receives an exclusive licence to distribute the Self-Service Portal under the Pulse Fitness brand.

## 3. Proposed Commercial Terms

### 3.1 Lite Portal — Licence Fee

Pulse Fitness pays a per-site monthly licence fee for each gym using the Lite portal.

| Metric | Terms |
|---|---|
| **Licence model** | Per-site, per-month |
| **Suggested price to Pulse Fitness** | £3 – £5 per gym site / month |
| **Pulse Fitness customer price** | Bundled free with equipment (value-add) |
| **Minimum commitment** | 12-month rolling agreement |
| **Volume discount** | Negotiable at 200+ sites |

> **Example:** 500 Pulse Fitness gym clients × £4/month = **£24,000/year** to GymAxis AI.
> Pulse Fitness benefits from increased customer retention, reduced support calls, and a competitive differentiator — at no cost to their end customer.

### 3.2 Pro Portal — Revenue Share

Pro subscriptions are sold jointly. GymAxis AI receives a recurring commission on all Pro revenue.

| Metric | Terms |
|---|---|
| **Suggested end-customer price** | £49 – £99 per gym site / month |
| **GymAxis AI commission** | 25% of gross monthly revenue |
| **Billing & collection** | Managed by Pulse Fitness |
| **Commission payment** | Monthly, within 30 days of collection |
| **Minimum term per subscriber** | 3-month initial commitment |

> **Example:** 100 gyms upgrade to Pro at £79/month = £94,800/year gross.
> GymAxis AI commission (25%) = **£23,700/year**.
> Combined with Lite licence = **£47,700/year total**.

### 3.3 Scaling Projections

| Year | Lite Sites | Pro Sites (est. 15-20% conversion) | GymAxis AI Annual Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 300 | 45 | £14,400 + £10,692 = **£25,092** |
| Year 2 | 600 | 120 | £28,800 + £28,512 = **£57,312** |
| Year 3 | 1,000 | 250 | £48,000 + £59,400 = **£107,400** |

*Assumes £4/month Lite licence, £79/month Pro price, 25% commission.*
