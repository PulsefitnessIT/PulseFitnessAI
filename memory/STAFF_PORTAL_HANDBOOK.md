# Pulse Fitness Staff Portal — Employee Handbook

_Keep this document updated whenever new portal features ship. It is the source
content for the downloadable PDF handbook (`GET /api/new-starter/portal/handbook.pdf`)._

## 1. Signing in
- Open the Staff Portal link in your welcome email and sign in with the details
  provided. On a phone the portal works like an app — add it to your home screen.
- After login you choose the **Staff Portal** or other modules from the launcher.

## 2. Getting around (mobile)
- A fixed **bottom bar** gives you the main areas: **Home**, **My Time**,
  **Holidays**, **Vehicle**, and **More** (Rota, Timesheets, Documents, System Access).
- The **bell** (top right) shows reminders and updates; tap an item to jump to it.

## 3. My Day (Home)
- Record your day in seconds: start/finish, breaks, working from home, sick, etc.
- Return-to-work sign-off: if you've been off sick, confirm the agreed actions here.

## 4. My Time
- Review your week or month of daily records and submit your timesheet for the period.
- A reminder appears if any days are missing confirmed hours.

## 5. Holidays
- Request holiday by picking your dates — the portal auto-calculates the **working
  days** (excluding weekends and bank holidays) and shows what's left of your allowance.
- Requests go to your line manager to approve; you're notified of the decision.

## 6. Vehicle checks (drivers)
- If a vehicle is assigned to you, complete its **weekly check** in the **Vehicle** tab.
- Your name, registration and vehicle details are pre-filled. Mark each item
  **OK / Defect / N/A**. The checklist matches your vehicle type (car, electric car,
  van, lorry).
- If anything is a **Defect** (or not safe to drive), describe it, add a photo, and
  submit — your manager is alerted automatically.
- Tick the declaration and **Submit**. Checks are due each week (Friday); you'll be
  reminded if one is outstanding.

## 7. Documents & System Access
- View your documents and the platform modules you have access to.

## Need help?
Contact your line manager or the Pulse HR team.
