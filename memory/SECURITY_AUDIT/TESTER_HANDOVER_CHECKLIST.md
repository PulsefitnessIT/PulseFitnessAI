## 5 · Out-of-scope (do not test these)

* Live production tenant `https://gymaxisai.com` and any data within.
* Emergent platform infrastructure (K8s, ingress, supervisor).
* Sub-processor infrastructure (Stripe, Resend, OpenAI, Anthropic,
  Google, Sentry, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudflare).
* Customer-owned integrated systems (a customer's Sage account, their
  Outlook tenant, etc.). If you can pivot **from** the GymAxis
  integration *into* the customer's system, that **is** in scope —
  but the customer's system itself is not your target.
* Physical and social-engineering tests.
* DoS / volumetric / amplification tests.

---

## 6 · Safe testing rules

* **No data exfiltration.** Proof-of-concept records only.
  Screenshots OK. Do not download / archive whole tenants.
* **No persistent backdoors.** No web shells. No reverse shells.
* **No defacement.** No content changes beyond what's necessary for
  a documented PoC.
* **Honour the do-not-lock list.** The Master Admin account
  (`admin@gymaxisai.com`) may be brute-force tested for ≤ 5 min,
  then must be released. All other accounts are fair game.
* **Use the staging tenant only.** Cross-tenant testing is in scope
  *within* staging; do not pivot to production.
* **If you find a real-world exploit, stop and call.** If you can
  read another tenant's data, exfiltrate a key, or persist a
  payload — pause testing and call the P0 phone immediately.

## 8 · Reporting format

Per finding, please include:

* **Title** — short, descriptive.
* **CVSS v3.1 score** with vector string.
* **OWASP category** (OWASP Top 10 or OWASP LLM Top 10 for AI
  findings).
* **Reproduction steps** — copy-pasteable curl / HTTP / browser
  steps. Place real credentials only inside the 1Password vault,
  not in the report PDF.
* **Evidence** — Burp HTTP log, curl + response, screenshot.
* **Recommended fix** — code-level sketch where possible.
* **References** — CWE, OWASP, CVE if applicable.

Severity rubric:

| Severity | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Remote code execution, full tenant takeover, master-admin compromise, mass-PII leak. |
| **High** | Cross-tenant data access, auth bypass, IDOR on sensitive resource, AI excessive agency without approval, payment bypass. |
| **Medium** | Information disclosure of business data (not PII), DOM-XSS in a low-privilege view, SSRF to internal CIDR, brute-force resistance gap. |
| **Low** | Missing security header, verbose error, weak TLS cipher, minor UX-level issue. |

---

## 9 · Emergency stop / escalation

* **Soft pause:** Slack message in the shared channel.
  Acknowledgement within 30 min during permitted hours.
* **Hard stop (P0):** Call the P0 phone (1Password). The on-call
  engineer will:
  1. Acknowledge within 30 min.
  2. Pause the engagement.
  3. Triage / contain (revoke tokens, rotate secrets, take a
     snapshot).
  4. Coordinate restart with the vendor lead.
* **Trigger conditions:**
  * Vendor accidentally accesses a record that may be live customer
    data.
  * A side-effect propagates beyond the staging tenant (e.g.
    real SMS, real email to a real recipient).
  * Vendor's tooling is causing unintended outages.

---

## 10 · Retest process

* Critical / High findings: **one round of fixed-price retest within
  60 days** of the report being delivered. Schedule via the same
  Slack channel.
* Medium / Low findings: re-tested by GymAxis internally + signed off
  by `security@gymaxisai.com`.
