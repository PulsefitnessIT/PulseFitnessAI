## 5. Why she still sees zero (row-level visibility)
- Her CRM role = `staff` → NOT master-admin and NOT in ("admin","owner"), so `compute_visibility` gives her **"team"** scope, not "all".
- `crm_sharing_rules` for {organization_id: 3ece2d0c, entity_type: "company"} = **0 rows** — nothing upgrades staff to "all".
- `crm2_companies` in her org WHERE `owner_id == e4a8085b...` (her id) = **0**. The 4 companies are owned by other users (`6fe053e0...`, `cb5487b8...`, `iso-owner`, `iso-manager`), none of which are Honey or (presumably) her reports.
- ⇒ Team/owner scope over zero owned/reportee-owned companies = **empty list**. Expected behavior.

## Fix options (owner to choose — no data changed by me)
1. **Elevate her CRM role** to `admin` or `owner` in `crm_users` (org 3ece2d0c) → grants "all" visibility across the org's companies. Note her tokens were last revoked with reason `role_or_permissions_changed`; a role change will require re-login.
2. **Add a `crm_sharing_rules` row** `{organization_id: 3ece2d0c, entity_type: "company", role: "staff", access: "all"}` → grants ALL staff in that org full company visibility (broader blast radius).
3. **Assign ownership** of the relevant companies to Honey (set `owner_id` = `e4a8085b...`) or make the current owners her reports → keeps her at team scope but gives her the specific accounts.

Recommend option 1 if she should see the whole book, or option 3 if she should only see a subset.
